Who we are

Stability. Commitment. Reliability.

Profile House has been working in the aluminium industry for several years. Our profiles serve key sectors of the economy.

We design, manufacture and supply profiles made from modern aluminium alloys — standard or to the client’s drawing, together with finishing, machining and assembly. We keep a close eye on the market and talk to our clients, so we respond quickly to their needs and take part in many projects.

We keep improving so we can offer clients ready-made solutions and grow together with them.

PROFILE HOUSEPH-06/101:1

Just-in-Time logistics

You receive exactly as much material as your production needs at any given moment.

Deliveries are more frequent but smaller. You need less warehouse space, react faster to changes in demand and don’t tie up money in unused material. We achieve this through fully synchronised production and close cooperation with suppliers.

How we make it work:

  • We apply group technology
  • We’ve cut waiting and processing times
  • We’ve eliminated potential causes of defects
  • We service our machinery regularly
